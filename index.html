Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to PixelHive. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our store and purchase digital products.

2. Information We Collect

We collect your name, email address, phone number, and payment proof screenshots when you place an order. This information is necessary to deliver your purchased digital products and process your payment verification.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To process orders and deliver digital files
  • To verify EasyPaisa / JazzCash payment screenshots
  • To send order confirmations and download links via email
  • To improve our products and customer experience
  • To respond to your queries and support requests

4. Data Sharing

We never sell or rent your personal information. Payment details are only shared with our payment processors (EasyPaisa, JazzCash) to verify transactions. Screenshots are deleted after verification is complete.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. All payment screenshots are stored on encrypted servers and access is restricted to authorized staff only.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal data at any time. Simply contact us at hello@pixelhive.shop or WhatsApp +92 348 0103280 to make a request.

7. Cookies

Our website uses essential cookies to maintain your shopping cart and session. We do not use third-party tracking cookies for advertising.
